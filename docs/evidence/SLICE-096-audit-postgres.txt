notes
-----
- Each tenant has its own chain and its own advisory lock; the sequence starts
  at 1 per tenant and concurrent writers do not interleave.
- The tamper test changed a field without touching the stored hash, and the
  verifier reported the exact tenant and sequence.
- Review fix: a row whose `event` JSON no longer parses used to surface as
  `AuditStoreUnavailable` from the generic handler. It now raises
  `AuditChainBroken`, and `test_corrupt_event_detected_as_chain_break` pins it.
- The dynamic WHERE clause only interpolates fixed column fragments and `$n`
  placeholders; values stay bound. Bandit B608 flags the concatenation, so the
  line carries a `# nosec B608` with that reason.
- Verification is fail-closed across the whole table: a row signed with a key
  the verifier does not hold fails the run. A leftover live-run row signed with
  a different key did fail the test suite until it was removed, which is the
  intended behavior for one platform-wide keyring.
- `query_audit_records` opens and closes its own pool for one-off callers;
  long-lived wiring in SLICE-097 should use `PostgresAuditQuery` or a sink that
  keeps the pool.
