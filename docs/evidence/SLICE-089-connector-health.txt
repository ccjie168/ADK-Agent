notes
-----
- `down089` points at a closed loopback port; Windows reports the refusal after
  about two seconds, which is why its latency is near the 5 second cap path.
- The console and CLI probes run from their own process. With `--registry-url`
  the spec is fetched over HTTP but the probe still leaves the operator's host.
- The check sends no credentials, resolves no `auth_ref`, reads no response
  body, and caps the timeout at 5 seconds.
- `test_redirect_not_followed_by_a_following_client` pins `follow_redirects=False`
  on the request itself, so an injected client that follows redirects cannot
  change the verdict.
